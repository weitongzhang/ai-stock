# Pond Quality Guide

## Purpose

Use this guide before selecting stocks. The goal is to avoid spending attention in places where there are few fish.

The sequence is:

```text
fish pond quality -> core stock -> mapping chain -> candidate -> trigger
```

## Pond Classes

### 富矿池

Use when most are true:

- Theme has repeated money flow across multiple sessions.
- There is at least one recognized core stock with high turnover or market identity.
- Sector breadth exists: front row, middle army, and laggards can be identified.
- Narrative is simple and can be explained in one sentence.
- Volume is large enough for active funds.
- Pullbacks produce承接 instead of immediate collapse.

Action: actively maintain core, middle-army, and mapping candidates. Wait for price triggers.

### 活跃池

Use when:

- Theme is strong today or recently, but persistence is not fully proven.
- There are 1-2 front-row stocks and some peer movement.
- Candidate mapping is logical but not yet broadly recognized.

Action: observe and use smaller size. Require stronger trigger confirmation.

### 观察池

Use when:

- Logic is interesting, but money flow is weak or only one stock is moving.
- Relationship exists, but the market has not recognized it.
- The theme may become important later but is not today's main pond.

Action: put into watchlist, no active trade unless price/volume changes.

### 贫瘠池

Use when:

- No recognized core stock.
- Weak turnover and no breadth.
- Only vague concept association.
- Repeated failures after catalysts.
- Sector is in退潮.

Action: downgrade or remove. Do not force a trade.

## Scoring

Score pond quality out of 20:

- Money flow and turnover: 5
- Recognized core stock: 5
- Breadth and chain structure: 4
- Persistence across sessions: 3
- Narrative clarity: 2
- Pullback承接: 1

Classification:

- 17-20: 富矿池
- 13-16: 活跃池
- 8-12: 观察池
- 0-7: 贫瘠池

## Practical Rules

- In a富矿池, a good candidate can be tracked through分歧.
- In an活跃池, require price confirmation before acting.
- In an观察池, only build the research file; do not rush.
- In a贫瘠池, relationship logic is not enough.
