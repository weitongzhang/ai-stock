# Long-Term Mapping Watch Pool

## Purpose

Maintain validated mapping chains across days and weeks. Do not rely only on today's top gainers.

The watch pool answers:

- Which relationship chains have already been recognized by the market?
- Is the core stock still creating辨识度, even if it is分歧 today?
- Is the mapping candidate承接,补涨,横盘蓄势, or失效?
- Which chains should remain in tomorrow's observation list?

## Chain States

Use one of these states:

- `活跃`: Core remains strong and mapping candidate is rising or preparing to break out.
- `核心分歧-映射承接`: Core is down or分歧, but mapping candidate holds/rises with volume. This is a high-value tracking state.
- `降温`: Both core and mapping candidate weaken, but support and narrative have not fully failed.
- `降级`: Chain still has a valid relationship, but current price/volume, sector state, or core strength is not actionable.
- `观察期`: Chain failed once or is close to failing, but relationship strength is high enough to review one more time.
- `失效`: Core breaks down, mapping candidate fails, or relationship is no longer being recognized.

## Keep/Downgrade Rules

Keep a chain if:

- Core is still high-volume and recognizable.
- Mapping candidate is holding support or absorbing volume.
- The sector remains active.
- The relationship is official or widely accepted.

Downgrade a chain if:

- Core loses recognition for several sessions.
- Mapping candidate repeatedly fails to respond.
- Sector breadth disappears.
- Candidate becomes high-volume stagnant near resistance.
- Relationship still exists but is no longer the current market pricing path.

Invalidate a chain if:

- Core breaks key support with heavy volume.
- Mapping candidate breaks its key platform after failed recognition.
- The relationship evidence proves immaterial or not accepted by the market.

## Negative Marking Rules

Always keep a visible negative list during long-term tracking:

- `降级`: Keep in watch pool but remove from active candidates.
- `观察期`: Review once more at the next key session; remove if no repair appears.
- `剔除`: Delete from active watch pool and keep only as historical memory.

Use this reason taxonomy:

- `core-faded`: Core stock lost辨识度 or entered退潮.
- `candidate-no-carry`: Mapping candidate failed to承接 while the core/sector was strong.
- `failed-breakout`: Candidate broke down after a mapped breakout attempt.
- `weak-relationship`: Relationship is too weak, indirect, or not market-recognized.
- `sector-faded`: Sector breadth disappeared.
- `overextended`: Candidate is no longer a catch-up; it became the high-risk front row.
- `liquidity-bad`: Liquidity, spread, or market cap makes the trade unreliable.
- `narrative-stale`: The story was already priced and no new confirmation appears.

## Re-activation Rules

A downgraded or removed chain can be reactivated only when at least two are true:

- Core stock regains high-volume strength.
- Mapping candidate reclaims the failed level with volume.
- Sector returns to the top market-flow group.
- New official evidence strengthens the relationship.
- The candidate forms a new low-risk platform after cooling.
