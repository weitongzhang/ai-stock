# Secondary Strategy Boundary

## Principle

Mapping catch-up is a secondary strategy. It should sit on top of:

```text
pond quality + recognized core stock + relationship evidence + price/volume confirmation
```

A relationship alone is not a buy signal.

## Upgrade Path

Use this state ladder:

1. `观察池`
   - Relationship exists.
   - Pond or core validation is incomplete.
   - No trade; keep tracking.

2. `候选观察`
   - Pond is at least活跃池.
   - Core stock has market recognition.
   - Candidate has not fully confirmed.
   - Wait for breakout, pullback承接, or volume recognition.

3. `重点观察`
   - Candidate itself confirms with volume/price.
   - Risk line is clear.
   - Sector/core still supports the setup.

4. `剔除`
   - Relationship is weak, pond is cold, or candidate repeatedly fails.

## Relationship Tiers

### Tier 1: Strong Mapping

- Parent/subsidiary.
- Spin-off listing.
- Consolidated subsidiary with material valuation.
- Direct controlling shareholder.

Default: `候选观察` if pond is active.

### Tier 2: Platform Mapping

- Same group.
- Same actual controller.
- Central SOE/local SOE asset platform.
- Important investee or sister company.

Default: `观察池`. Upgrade only after market recognition.

### Tier 3: Industrial Chain Mapping

- Supplier/customer.
- Upstream resource/downstream high-elasticity product.
- Equipment/materials link.

Default: `观察池` or `候选观察`, depending on pond strength and evidence.

### Tier 4: Concept Mapping

- Same concept label.
- Same region.
- Similar name.
- Vague narrative.

Default: `逻辑观察` or `剔除`.

## Example: 有研新材

有研新材 can be tracked as:

```text
有研集团 / 有研粉材 / 有研系新材料资产 -> 有研新材
```

Classification:

- Relationship: same-group/platform mapping.
- Default state: `观察池`.
- Upgrade condition: 有研系、新材料、稀土、半导体材料 pond becomes active, and有研新材 itself confirms with volume.
- Do not treat as strong parent-subsidiary mapping unless official ownership and market pricing both support it.

## Failure Rules

Downgrade or remove when:

- Pond remains cold after repeated catalysts.
- Core stock loses recognition.
- Candidate does not react while the pond is strong.
- Relationship turns out immaterial.
- Price breaks down after a failed recognition attempt.
