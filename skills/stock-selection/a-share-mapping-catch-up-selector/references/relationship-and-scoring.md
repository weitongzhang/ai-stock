# Relationship And Scoring Guide

## Relationship Strength Ladder

Score relationship strength out of 30:

- 28-30: Direct parent/subsidiary, consolidated subsidiary, spin-off listing, or controlling shareholder with material equity value.
- 24-27: Same actual controller or same group listed platform with clear asset/revenue connection.
- 20-23: Major equity holding, important investee, or disclosed strategic partnership with financial relevance.
- 16-19: Direct upstream/downstream relationship with product, capacity, customer, or supplier evidence.
- 11-15: Same industrial chain and clear peer validation, but no direct equity or supply evidence.
- 6-10: Same concept label only; use only as a weak watch item.
- 0-5: Narrative association is vague, stale, or not investable.

## Core Stock Strength

Score core strength out of 20:

- 18-20: Multi-day high recognition, high turnover, sector leadership, and continued breadth expansion.
- 15-17: Strong trend or limit-up/20cm recognition with at least one follow-through day.
- 11-14: Strong one-day move but persistence not proven.
- 6-10: News spike without sector confirmation.
- 0-5: Core has already broken down or lost recognition.

## Candidate Position

Score position/catch-up room out of 15:

- 13-15: Low/mid position, not extended, clear platform, good room to prior resistance.
- 10-12: Has moved but not exhausted; still below major resistance.
- 6-9: Already significantly extended or near heavy resistance.
- 0-5: Higher than the core's risk state or high-volume stagnant.

## Liquidity And Capacity

Score liquidity/capacity out of 10:

- 8-10: Suitable market cap and turnover for both institutions and active funds.
- 5-7: Tradable but liquidity may limit follow-through.
- 0-4: Too illiquid, too small, or too constrained for reliable mapping.

## Price/Volume Trigger

Score trigger quality out of 15:

- 13-15: Volume breakout or confirmed pullback with sector/core resonance.
- 10-12: Early volume recognition, but trigger still incomplete.
- 6-9: Relationship is logical but price has not confirmed.
- 0-5: Weak trend, breakdown, or failed breakout.

## Catalyst Clarity

Score catalyst/narrative out of 10:

- 8-10: Market can explain the mapping in one sentence, and evidence is official or widely recognized.
- 5-7: Narrative is understandable but needs more proof.
- 0-4: Too complex, obscure, or not accepted by the market.

## True Mapping Checklist

Prefer candidates where at least three are true:

- The core stock has already created large space or strong辨识度.
- The relationship is official, financial, or industrial rather than only同概念.
- Candidate has lower relative position or better capacity than the core.
- Candidate starts to show independent volume after the core's move.
- The sector has breadth rather than only one isolated stock.
- The mapping can be explained simply.

## False Mapping Red Flags

Reject or downgrade when:

- The relationship is only name similarity, location similarity, or vague concept tagging.
- Candidate does not react after repeated core strength.
- Candidate's business exposure is tiny relative to total revenue/assets.
- Core stock is already in退潮.
- Candidate is already high and only using the core as an after-the-fact story.

## Example Patterns

### Subsidiary/Spin-Off Revaluation

Core: subsidiary or spin-off makes a large move.

Mapping candidate: parent company or controlling platform.

Focus:

- Parent's ownership/economic exposure.
- Whether subsidiary valuation is material relative to parent market cap.
- Parent's own trend and liquidity.

### Elastic Materials Or Equipment Core

Core: high-elasticity material/equipment stock becomes a thematic front row.

Mapping candidate: upstream resource platform, larger-cap equipment leader, key supplier/customer, or low-position peer.

Focus:

- Whether the candidate can be understood as a capacity carrier.
- Whether the market wants lower-volatility exposure after the core becomes crowded.

### Resource Platform Catch-Up

Core: downstream high-elasticity stock surges.

Mapping candidate: upstream resource or smelting/processing platform with strategic exposure.

Focus:

- Commodity price trend.
- Candidate's own resource cycle logic.
- Whether the downstream core is strong enough to add a valuation option rather than becoming the only reason to buy.
