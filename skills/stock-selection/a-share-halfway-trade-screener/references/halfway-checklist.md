# Halfway Trade Checklist

## Environment Gate

- Index is not in a fast intraday selloff.
- Market has at least one clear mainline or sector cluster.
- Limit-up and high-gain stocks are not rapidly failing.
- Candidate sector has front-row examples, not isolated movement.

If the environment gate fails, do not continue screening.

## Sector Gate

Score sector strength by:

- Number of涨停/大涨 stocks.
- Whether a中军 or high-liquidity stock is also rising.
- Whether the sector rises with volume, not only one small-cap stock.
- Whether the sector is aligned with the market's current main narrative.

Prefer sectors ranked top 1-3 intraday.

## Individual Stock Gate

Required:

- Price is above intraday average/VWAP.
- Pullbacks hold and lows lift.
- Volume expands on upswings and contracts on pullbacks.
- Stock is in the front row of its sector by gain, liquidity, or leadership.
- Stop loss is close.

Reject:

- Long upper shadow after high-volume spike.
- Falling below average price repeatedly.
- Up only because of a single influencer/media item.
- A late rear-row follower after leaders already exhausted.

## Volume Reading

Healthy:

- Today's成交额 is meaningfully higher than recent average.
- Price advances with volume and rests with lower volume.
- Turnover is enough for liquidity but not so high that it shows exhaustion.

Risky:

- 成交额 suddenly explodes but price stops rising.
- High turnover after multi-day surge.
- Repeated failed breaks of intraday high.

## Execution Template

- First buy after VWAP/pullback confirmation: 0.5-1成.
- Add only after new high confirmation and sector strengthening.
- If price fails after entry, exit. Do not wait for closing confirmation.
- If the stock seals涨停, decide whether to hold based on封单,板块强度, and market environment.
- If it cannot seal and keeps expanding volume, take profit or cut quickly.
